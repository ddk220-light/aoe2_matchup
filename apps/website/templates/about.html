How AoE2 Matchup works

The data and the simulation behind every stat, score, and matchup.

AoE2 Matchup is a free, fan-made analysis suite for Age of Empires II: Definitive Edition. It turns the game's own data into battle simulations, unit rankings, and civilization breakdowns you can explore.

Where the data comes from

Every unit's stats are extracted directly from the game's binary data file (the .dat) — the same source the game itself reads. We then compute fully-upgraded Imperial-age stats for all 56 civilizations (including every unique unit and the Three Kingdoms civs), applying all the blacksmith, university and unique-technology upgrades a civilization can reach, plus its civ bonuses. So the numbers you see are what a unit actually looks like late-game for that civ — not the base stat card.

How a matchup is decided

Who wins isn't guessed from a formula — it's simulated. A deterministic, tick-by-tick model fights the units against each other, accounting for armor classes, attack reload and delays, projectile travel time and accuracy, charge attacks, trample, bleed, and other unit mechanics. The finalized land rankings use 7,600 selected runs for 555 civilization/unit variants across 56 civilizations, covering 2,398 distinct matchups. Results are stored so the tables load instantly.

What the scores mean

Land rankings balance population and resource efficiency: the cheaper side fields 27 units; the more expensive side fields round(27 × sqrt(cheaper cost / expensive cost)). Weighted cost uses food 1, wood 0.9, and gold 1.1. There are no buffer units.

Each unit faces its group's standard opponents. A win contributes its surviving HP percentage, a loss contributes the negative enemy surviving HP percentage, and a draw contributes zero. Average three seeded runs per matchup, or ten when the first three outcomes differ, then give each opponent equal weight. Score = 50 + half the average signed HP percentage. Scores range from 0 to 100; 50 is neutral. There is no extra speed, range or cost multiplier.

The finalized dataset combines completed results from the original and corrected Simulation V3 engines; source versions are recorded with the data. Siege and naval tables retain their earlier scoring methods.

The tools

  • Battle Simulator — pit any units from any civs against each other and watch the fight.
  • Unit Rankings — every unit scored and ranked across all civs.
  • Civilizations — each civ's strategic identity and strongest units.
  • Matchup Advisor — what to build against a given civ.
  • Unit Matchups — head-to-head pages for every unique-unit pairing.
  • Patch Tracker — how each balance update changed the numbers.

Keeping current

When Age of Empires II ships a balance patch, the stats and simulations are re-derived and the Patch Tracker shows exactly which units and matchups moved.

Frequently asked

Is this an official Microsoft site?
No — it's a free fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Microsoft, Forgotten Empires, or World's Edge.
Why might a result differ from my own game?
Simulations assume fully-upgraded Imperial-age units fighting head-on; real games add micro, terrain, and tech timing the sim doesn't model.
