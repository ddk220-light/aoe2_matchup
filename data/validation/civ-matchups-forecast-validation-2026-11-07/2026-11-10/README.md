# Final completed forecast evidence: one approved production update

The owner explicitly authorized updating the matchup table, committing and pushing the result, and deploying to production before starting separate recovery work on the 36 unfinished contexts. `authorization.json` preserves that instruction. This is one release; recurring tasks remain disabled and future automatic releases are not authorized.

The unchanged research snapshot 070 contains **39,112 reviewed contexts**: **31,895 single-seed confirmations** and **7,217 multi-seed validations**. **3,476 outcomes changed** and **35,636 retained** the frozen forecast. **1,081 contexts have mixed native winners** across ten distinct seeds. Mixed results are a subset of those comparisons, not an additional group. Agreement-dependent stopping cannot estimate unbiased global forecast accuracy.
