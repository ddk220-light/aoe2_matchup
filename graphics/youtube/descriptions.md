## Kona vs ALL Unique Units

The Mapuche Kona charges into every unique unit in Age of Empires II — one lance, one
macana, no mercy. We simulated all 60+ of them at equal numbers AND equal resources, and
the close calls are brutal.

The Kona fights the Bengali Ratha to a literal dead heat, loses to the Byzantine Cataphract
by a hair, and flips a coin with the Frankish Throwing Axeman. The wildest ones depend on how
you count it: against Eagles, Champi Warriors and the Chu Ko Nu the Kona wins the even-numbers
brawl but loses once you pay for it gold-for-gold — and the Hussite Wagon does the exact
opposite. Then the Teutonic Knight and Boyar just delete it. 💀

## Blackwood Archer vs ALL Unique Units

Tupi's Blackwood Archer might be the ultimate glass cannon. Man-for-man it kites and shreds
almost every unique unit in the game — but it costs a fortune, so what happens when the gold
has to balance?

The honest answer: equal numbers, it's terrifying (it laughs at the Samurai, Kamayuk, Obuch
and even the Viking Berserk). Equal resources, it gets humbled — most melee units simply
out-value it. The genuinely even fights are the mirror-match Ibirapema Warrior, the Khitan
Liao Dao, and the Ethiopian Shotel Warrior, and it scores real cost-wins against the Teutonic
Knight, the Fire Lancer and the Arambai. Against war machines like the Ballista Elephant and
War Wagon? Don't watch. 😬

## Guecha Warrior vs ALL Unique Units

The Muisca Guecha Warrior — atlatl in hand, skulls on his back — takes on every unique unit
in Age of Empires II. We simulated all of them at equal numbers and equal resources, and this
one trades blows all the way down the roster.

The closest fights are pure nail-biters: a coin-flip with the Viking Berserk, an even split
both ways with the Berber Camel Archer, and tense, slightly-losing battles against the
Vietnamese Fire Lancer, the Mongol Steppe Lancer and the Byzantine Cataphract. Several units
(Liao Dao, Karambit Warrior, Samurai, Serjeant) crumble to the Guecha at equal numbers but
turn the tables on cost. Watch out for the Hussite Wagon and Goth Huskarl, though. ☠️
