## Blackwood Archer vs ALL Unique Units

Tupi's Blackwood Archer might be the ultimate glass cannon. Man-for-man it kites and shreds
almost every unique unit in the game — but it costs a fortune, so what happens when the gold
has to balance?

The honest answer: equal numbers, it's terrifying (it laughs at the Samurai, Kamayuk, Obuch
and even the Viking Berserk). Equal resources, it gets humbled — most melee units simply
out-value it. The genuinely even fights are the mirror-match Ibirapema Warrior, the Khitan
Liao Dao, and the Ethiopian Shotel Warrior, and it scores real cost-wins against the Teutonic
Knight, the Fire Lancer and the Arambai. Against war machines like the Ballista Elephant and
War Wagon? Don't watch. 😬

## Temple Guard vs ALL Unique Units

The Muisca Temple Guard doesn't hit hard — it hits FASTER. Every swing shaves its reload
time down until it's attacking twice as often as it started, so we sent it into all 64
unique units in Age of Empires II at equal numbers AND equal resources to see how far a
gold mask and a ceremonial staff actually gets you.

It finishes almost dead level: 33-31 at equal numbers, 30-33 once the gold has to balance.
The closest fights are genuinely tense — narrow losses to the Khitan Liao Dao, the Italian
Genoese Crossbowman and its own civ-mate the Guecha Warrior, a clean win over the Tupi
Blackwood Archer, and a dead-heat coin-flip with the Wu Fire Archer on cost. Four units

the tables once you pay gold-for-gold — while the Persian War Elephant and the Mounted
Trebuchet do the exact opposite. Most cavalry melts to it, though the Cataphract and the
Mameluke very much don't. And gunpowder? Don't watch: the Jurchen Grenadier and Portuguese
Organ Gun are as one-sided as this simulator gets. 💀

## Champi Warrior — Unit Matchup Guide

The Champi Warrior is the cheapest mace in the Americas: 50 food, 25 gold, quick feet, four
pierce armour, and an attack that punches straight through the man in front. We ran the fully
upgraded Elite version (Inca) against all 65 unique units in Age of Empires II at equal
numbers AND equal resources — and the two answers could hardly be further apart.

Man-for-man it gets battered: 11 wins to 49 losses. Pay gold-for-gold and it becomes a
completely different unit — 22 wins, 35 losses and 8 dead heats — purely because you field so
many more of them. Ten matchups flip outright from loss to win once cost is counted, nearly
all of them expensive kit: the Mangudai, Mameluke, Conquistador, War Wagon, Organ Gun,
Ballista Elephant and Mounted Trebuchet each win the fair fight and lose the economic one.
That pierce armour also makes it a real archer-eater — the Genitour and the Genoese
Crossbowman never get going. The razor-thin ones are the Shrivamsha Rider, Chakram Thrower,
Ghulam and Plumed Archer, all inside single digits either way. Zero melee armour is the
catch: the Teutonic Knight and the Boyar simply delete it, and the Jurchen Grenadier is the
single most one-sided fight in the entire run. 💀

## Ibirapema Warrior — Unit Matchup Guide

The Tupi Ibirapema Warrior swings one enormous two-handed club and wears nothing at all. We
ran the Elite version against all 64 unique units at equal numbers AND equal resources, and
the honest answer is that it is one of the roughest rides in the game: 9 wins to 55 losses
man-for-man, and 11–52 once gold has to balance.

Where it does work, it works on other infantry — it beats the White Feather Guard, the Jian
Swordsman and the Kamayuk comfortably, and edges the Genitour, Shotel Warrior and Huskarl.
The genuinely close ones are the Magyar Huszar and the Woad Raider, both narrow losses. But
anything that shoots or rolls simply erases it: the Grenadier, Organ Gun, Mounted Trebuchet,
War Chariot, Camel Archer and Plumed Archer are all in the −97 to −100 range, about as
one-sided as this simulator goes. Two matchups do rescue themselves on cost — the Arambai
and the Mounted Trebuchet both flip from loss to win — and the mirror against its own civ's
Blackwood Archer swings from a −78 thrashing to a dead heat once you pay for it. 💀

## Slinger — Unit Matchup Guide

The Slinger is the Andean answer to a wall of infantry: cheap, ranged, and carrying a
bonus that turns swordsmen into gravel. We ran the Imperial version (Inca) against all 65
unique units at equal numbers AND equal resources.

It finishes 20–42–3 man-for-man and a much healthier 27–35–3 on cost, and the shape of the

wheeled it is the reverse: the War Wagon, Mounted Trebuchet, Tarkan, Tiger Cavalry and
Hussite Wagon all sit around −90. The real coin-flips are the Woad Raider, the Huskarl and
the Gbeto, all inside ten points. Seven matchups flip from loss to win once gold balances,
including the Cataphract, the Mameluke and the Janissary. 🪨

## Warrior Priest — Unit Matchup Guide

## Fire Archer — Unit Matchup Guide

The Wu Fire Archer throws flaming dragon-head arrows and looks terrifying. In a straight
one-on-one block fight it is, statistically, the worst unit in this whole series: 4 wins to
54 losses at equal numbers, 9–51 on cost, across all 64 unique units.

That is the point, though — it is an area-damage support unit, not a duelist, and the
close-run fights show where the value hides. It is dead level with the Samurai, the
Sicilian Serjeant and the Khitan Liao Dao, and it is level with the Teutonic Knight
man-for-man before turning that into a 64-point win once you pay for the Teuton. The
Karambit Warrior swings even harder: a 28-point loss becomes a 77-point win on cost. Its
only outright wins are against slow infantry — the Urumi Swordsman, Kamayuk, Ibirapema
Warrior and Obuch. Anything with wheels or armour ends it: Mounted Trebuchet, Hussite
Wagon, Ballista Elephant and War Wagon are all −94 or worse. 🔥

## Iron Pagoda — Unit Matchup Guide

The Jurchen Iron Pagoda is a wall of lamellar on an armoured horse, and man-for-man it is
the most dominant unit in this entire series. Against all 64 unique units at equal numbers
it goes 51 wins, 9 losses, 4 dead heats. Almost nothing beats it in a fair fight.

Then you count the gold. At equal resources it drops to 38–18–8, and ten matchups flip
outright from win to loss: the Obuch, White Feather Guard, Serjeant, Berserk, Chu Ko Nu,
Liao Dao, Composite Bowman, Jaguar Warrior and Bolas Rider all beat it once you can field
more of them. That is the Iron Pagoda in one line — you are not paying for wins, you are
paying for *certainty*. It farms the Genitour, Ghulam, Blackwood Archer and Fire Archer. The
handful of units that beat it straight up are the War Elephant, Mameluke, Teutonic Knight
and Boyar, and the Genoese Crossbowman punishes it hardest on cost at −72. 🛡️

## White Feather Guard — Unit Matchup Guide

The Shu White Feather Guard is the clearest example in this whole series of why equal
numbers is a misleading way to compare units. Man-for-man it goes 10 wins to 52 losses
against all 64 unique units. Judged that way it looks like one of the worst units in the
game.

Pay gold-for-gold and it becomes 26–34–4, and **fifteen** matchups flip outright from loss
to win — the biggest cost swing of any unit here. The Hussite Wagon (−91 to +23), the
Conquistador (−90 to +35), the Mangudai (−88 to +12), the War Wagon, Mounted Trebuchet,
Ballista Elephant, War Elephant, Mameluke, Centurion, Tarkan, Iron Pagoda, Tiger Cavalry,
Karambit Warrior, Guecha Warrior and Ratha all lose to it once you can field the numbers.
It beats the Genitour by 95 in both modes. What still ends it regardless: the Grenadier,
Organ Gun, Teutonic Knight and Composite Bowman. 🪶
