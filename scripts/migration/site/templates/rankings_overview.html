Rankings at a glance

Land units are scored by Simulation V3 at full Imperial upgrades, with 27 units on the cheaper side and population- and cost-balanced opposing armies. Each matchup averages three seeded runs, extended to ten when outcomes differ. Score = 50 + half the average signed surviving HP percentage. Higher is stronger; 50 is neutral.
